About Our Data:

This website is based on the American Time Use Survey (ATUS) data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics.

The ATUS is a survey that collects data on how people in the United States spend their time. The survey has been conducted multiple times. Our data is from Version 8 which was released on March 10, 2025.

The official dataset and further information can be found here: https://doi.org/10.3886/ICPSR36268.v8

Our analysis of the data uses subsets from 2022-2023 and 2012-2013. Our data is averaged by the age of participants in the survrey to offer more global and applicable results to users of the website.
