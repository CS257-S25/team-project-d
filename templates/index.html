This website can show you the top 3 activities for a certain age and allows you to compare the amount of time spent doing an activity for an age across decades. Further information on available data can be found on the Find Activities page.

This website is based on the American Time Use Survey (ATUS) data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics.The ATUS is a survey that collects data on how people in the United States spend their time.
